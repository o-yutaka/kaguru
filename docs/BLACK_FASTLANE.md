# BLACK Fast Lane

The fast lane is the cheap front-end to Kaggriculture research.

## Invariants
- The official Kaggriculture environment remains the authoritative truth.
- Fast-lane fingerprints, ranking, and preflight are advisory only.
- The fixed Phase3 production/task/plant/terminal machinery remains frozen.
- Expensive evaluation is reserved for survivors from the bounded screening matrix.
- Paired seeds and both seats are mandatory for measured promotion.

## Matrix

- 8 fixed seeds

## Adaptive budget
